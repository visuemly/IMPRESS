The concept of IMPRESS is to explore the graphic potential of a typeface through the connection between screen printing and an interactive digital specimen. Our personal interest in poster design led us to choose the Ruder Plakat typeface, whose expressive character, distinctive shapes and varying letter heights appealed to us the most. 

Since screen printing has a long-standing connection to poster culture, particularly through its use of bold colours, strong contrasts and repeatable prints, we chose it as the conceptual and visual foundation for our project.

We translate this traditional printing process into an interactive experience in which physical actions influence the digital type specimen. Through hand tracking and colour detection, users transform the typography with their movements and the colours of physical printing materials. Each downward printing gesture generates a new composition, demonstrating how the typeface can be continuously reinterpreted through form, movement and colour.
